## Catalog corrections and refreshed PNGs, 11 November 2026

Spyder Elephant is retired from active catalog listings and new rewards; its stable ID is retained only to resolve previously owned inventory. Headless Horseman is now 200,000 BU. The nine requested Secret items are repriced within 1–30 BU.

17 active icons were refreshed from transparent public wiki image files found during image research. Native PNG files are copied without modification. Exact asset URLs are recorded in `wiki-expansion/items.json`. Noobini Pizzanini, Graipuss Medussi, Chipso and Queso, Los Spaghettis, Los Sekolahs and Headless Horseman retain their verified transparent PNGs because the alternative search results contained background fragments, captions or opaque promotional artwork. Opaque images, illustrations with backgrounds and watermarked alternatives were excluded. Direct Google search was rate limited; available image search and source image files were used.
